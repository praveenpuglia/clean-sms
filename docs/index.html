Sorted

Every message, in its place

Personal, Transactions, Services, Offers and Government each get their own tab. Clean SMS reads the sender's registered TRAI header, so a sale never buries a message from your bank.

  • Sorted the moment a message arrives
  • Mute promotional notifications in one switch
  • An All tab when you want everything together
Recognisable

Know who's texting at a glance

Verified business senders show their logo, matched on your phone against India's DLT sender registry. Look-alike headers registered to someone else never get one.

OTPs

Codes that copy themselves, and clean up after

Every OTP lands in its own tab with the code pulled out. Tap to copy, or copy straight from the notification.

  • Mark as read right from any notification
  • Optionally delete OTPs after an hour, a day, or whatever suits you
  • Starred OTPs are always kept
Spam

Fight spam the way TRAI asks

Messages your operator labels as spam are flagged. Report any unwanted message to TRAI's 1909 in a couple of taps, written in the exact format TRAI expects and sent from the SIM that received it.
